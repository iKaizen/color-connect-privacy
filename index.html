Privacy Policy

Color Connect — IzeMonster

Last updated: September 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how IzeMonster ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in connection with the mobile game Color Connect (the "Game") on Android and iOS. By downloading or using the Game, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

We do not require you to create an account and we do not directly collect personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number. Your game progress is stored locally on your device.

The Game displays advertising provided by third parties. To serve ads, these providers may collect and process certain information automatically, including:

  • Advertising identifiers (Google Advertising ID / GAID on Android, IDFA on iOS)
  • Device information (device model, operating system version, language, country)
  • General, non-precise location derived from your IP address
  • Ad interaction data (ads shown, viewed, or completed)

2. Advertising & Third-Party Services

The Game uses Google AdMob to show rewarded video ads. AdMob is provided by Google and its use of data is governed by Google's own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • About AdMob and user data

3. In-App Purchases

The Game offers a one-time purchase, “Unlock All Levels” (also known as Remove Ads), processed entirely through Google Play Billing. We never see or store your card number, billing address, or other payment details — Google handles the transaction. The Game only receives a confirmation that the purchase is owned, stored on your device and tied to your Google Play account so it can be restored on reinstall or on another device. See Google Play Terms of Service.

4. Planned Features

Some data uses described here relate to features that may not be active in your installed version yet. This policy covers them in advance so its address does not change when they are introduced; a feature only collects data once it actually ships in a Game update.

  • Analytics (planned): a future version may include Google Firebase Analytics to understand aggregate usage (e.g. which levels are difficult). If enabled it may collect app-usage events, device information, and an app-instance identifier, used only to improve the Game and not to identify you personally. See Firebase Privacy & Security.
  • Cloud save (planned): a future version may sync your game progress across devices via Google Play Games Services, storing progress under your Google account. Sign-in is handled by Google; we never receive your Google credentials.

5. Consent (EEA, UK & Switzerland)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, we present a consent form (via Google's User Messaging Platform) before ads are personalized. You may choose whether to allow personalized advertising, and you can change your choice at any time in the Game's settings where available.

6. Your Choices & Opt-Out

You can limit ad tracking at any time through your device settings:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Delete/Reset advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization.
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising.

7. Children's Privacy

The Game is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under the age required by the laws of your country. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps to remove it.

8. Data Retention & Security

Game progress is stored only on your device and is removed when you uninstall the Game. Data processed by advertising partners is retained and secured according to their respective privacy policies.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the Game after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
